Penny the Parasaurolophus

Get to know Penny

Penny is a very loving and responsible dinosaur who loves to party. She's the oldest of four siblings so she often takes charge (she can sometimes be a bit bossy). She has a very kind heart and is always looking out for others. One thing many won't expect is that Penny's dream is to fly! But that's kind of hard when you're a dinosaur with no wings.

Birthday: June 5th

What Penny loves:

  • Her younger siblings
  • Parties
  • Giving gifts
  • Swimming

Why Penny wants to fly:

  • "I'll be free"
  • "I can see everything"
  • "Faster than walking"
