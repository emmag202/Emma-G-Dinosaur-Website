Rexy the T-Rex

Get to know Rexy

Rexy is a very social dinosaur. He loves making new friends with everyone he meets! He has a younger sister named Sue who he loves very much and is very protective of.

Birthday: August 12th

What Rexy loves:

  • His friends
  • Fantasy books
  • Making sure no one is left out
  • The hat his mom gave him for his birthday
  • His little sister

Rexy loves books, but has some trouble reading so he often listens to audiobooks instead.

Books Rexy loves:

  • Dragon Masters by Tracey West
  • Magic Tree House by Mary Pope Osborne
  • The Kingdom of Wrenly by Jordan Quinn
